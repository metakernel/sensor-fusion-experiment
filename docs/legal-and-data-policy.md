# Legal and Data Policy (Scaffold)

## Purpose
Track legal and compliance expectations for dataset use and model artifacts.

## Baseline Policy Placeholders
- Use only data covered by valid licenses and terms.
- Respect provider restrictions (redistribution, derivative use, access control).
- Do not commit restricted raw data, credentials, or tokens.
- Keep artifacts free of personally identifying content unless explicitly allowed.

## Operational Expectations
- Store credentials outside the repository.
- Gate data access with documented roles and approvals.
- Keep an audit trail for data source, version, and usage context.

## Next Steps
- Add project-specific legal references and approval owners.
- Document retention and deletion windows for raw and processed data.
- Add a review checklist for releases and external sharing.
